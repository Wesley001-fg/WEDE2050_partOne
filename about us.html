Who We Are

Spacebotics is an open-source, community-driven non-profit organization dedicated to developing advanced robotic systems for deep space research and mission support. Our focus is being cost-effective, autonomous platforms designed for the Moon, Mars, and beyond. We desire tocollaborate with universities, research institutions, and global space agencies to accelerate scientific discovery through robotic innovation.

WHAT WE DO:

Aiming to make space accessible to the world through specializing in autonomous affordable robots.


THE FACILITY:

Johannesburg Mondoer - Where all the software, hardware testing and development takes place.

Our Staging Facility

The facility in Johannesburg Mondoer serves as the baseline workshop environment where the core foundation builds and operates its lines of landers, planetary rovers, autonomous spacecraft navigation systems, and other cutting-edge space technologies.


Mission Control & Payload Tracking

Off-world hardware management is handled through a highly secure, real-time environment. Spacebotics payloads will be controlled directly from our Mission Control Center inside Rosebank International College, located at the Braamfontein Campus inside the 4th-floor computer lab.

Our engineering team has quick, uninterrupted communication access to track rovers, and launch commands from terminal when neededas they navigate on their missions.
